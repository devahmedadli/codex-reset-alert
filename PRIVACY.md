# Privacy Policy — Codex Reset Alert

**Last updated: October 13, 2026**

Codex Reset Alert is an independent browser extension developed by Ahmed Adli to provide notifications about publicly reported Codex reset activity.

## 1. Information We Collect

Codex Reset Alert does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personal information, browser history, Codex credentials, or private conversations.

The extension does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

## 2. Locally Stored Information

The extension uses browser local storage to save:

- Notification and sound preferences
- Preferred language and checking interval
- Cached public reset status
- Event identifiers to avoid duplicate notifications
- Request caching and retry information
- Notification source links

This information is used to provide the extension's functionality and is not uploaded to a developer-operated account or analytics service.

## 3. Third-Party Data Source

The extension periodically requests publicly available information from:

https://codex-resets.com/api/v1/status

The request uses HTTPS and does not include Codex credentials or authentication cookies.

As with ordinary web requests, the third-party service may receive connection metadata, including the requesting IP address and request headers, according to its own infrastructure and privacy practices.

The reset data is provided by an independent community tracker, not by OpenAI.

## 4. Notifications

Browser notifications are generated from newly detected public reset information. Optional audio notifications can be enabled or disabled through the extension's settings.

## 5. External Links

The extension may open external websites when users choose to view a source announcement, visit the tracker, or follow the optional donation link.

These websites operate under their respective privacy policies.

## 6. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell or share locally stored extension preferences or personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

The extension does not transmit locally stored settings or event history to a developer-operated server.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Extension settings and cached public information remain in browser local storage until removed or cleared.

Users can remove the extension and its locally stored data through their browser's extension management interface.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The extension is a general-purpose monitoring utility and is not specifically directed at children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available through the project's public repository.
